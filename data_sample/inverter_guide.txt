SOLAR AND HYBRID INVERTER SELECTION GUIDE
Version 1.0 — Department of Energy Systems
==========================================

1. INVERTER TYPES

String inverter: One inverter serves one or more series strings of
panels. It has the lowest cost per kW and is the simplest to maintain.
Shading on one panel reduces the output of the whole string.

Microinverter: One small inverter is mounted behind each panel.
Shading affects only the shaded panel. It has a higher cost per kW and
more units to service on the roof.

Hybrid inverter: Combines the PV inverter, a battery charger and a
backup-power output in one unit. It is required for any system that
stores energy in a battery.

Weighted (European) efficiency is 92-95% for residential units, which
is consistent with the 5-8% inverter loss used in the Installation
Guide. Peak efficiency quoted on datasheets is typically 2-3
percentage points higher than weighted efficiency.

2. MPPT AND STRING SIZING

The maximum power point tracker (MPPT) adjusts the operating voltage
of each string to extract the most power. A typical 5 kW residential
hybrid inverter has:

- MPPT voltage window: 120 V to 500 V
- Maximum DC input voltage: 600 V
- Number of MPPT inputs: 2
- Maximum input current per MPPT: 15 A

Open-circuit voltage (Voc) rises as panels get colder, by
approximately 0.3% per degree Celsius below 25 degrees. String Voc
must be calculated at the coldest expected site temperature and must
stay below the maximum DC input voltage.

3. DC/AC RATIO AND CLIPPING

The DC/AC ratio is the array rating in kWp divided by the inverter AC
rating in kW. A ratio between 1.1 and 1.3 is recommended. For example,
a 6 kW array on a 5 kW inverter has a ratio of 1.2. At a ratio of 1.2,
clipping losses are normally below 2% of annual yield. Above 1.3,
clipping losses become significant and the oversizing is not
recommended.

4. HYBRID INVERTER SPECIFICATION (5 kW CLASS)

- Rated AC output: 5 kW continuous, 230 V
- Surge capacity: 7.5 kW for 10 seconds
- Battery input: 51.2 V nominal (matches a 16S LiFePO4 pack)
- Maximum battery charge and discharge current: 100 A
- Backup changeover time: under 20 ms
- Enclosure rating: IP65, suitable for outdoor wall mounting
- Ambient operating range: -25 to 60 degrees C, with output
  derating above 40 degrees C
- Mounting clearance: at least 15 cm on all sides

5. ADDITIONAL FAULT CODES

Codes F-001 to F-005 are listed in the Installation and Maintenance
Guide. Hybrid inverters add the following codes:

Code  F-006: Battery communication lost. Check the CAN bus cable
            between the battery BMS and the inverter.
Code  F-007: Battery under-voltage. Pack voltage is below 44.8 V.
            Recharge from the grid or PV before reuse.
Code  F-008: Battery over-voltage. Pack voltage is above 58.4 V.
            Check BMS cell balancing.
Code  F-009: AC overload. The connected load exceeds the continuous
            rating. Disconnect the non-critical loads.

6. SERVICE LIFE

- Inverter design life: 10-15 years
- Cooling fans: inspect annually and replace every 7 years
- Firmware: check for updates once a year
